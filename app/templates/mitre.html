How this replaces hardcoded weights

Every value above is a lookup from config/severity_weights.json, generated once offline by tools/derive_severity.py against a pinned, hash-verified MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise bundle — never assigned by hand and never re-derived at scoring time. The scorer (app/cti/scorer.py) multiplies this severity by corroboration (how many feeds agree) and freshness (how recently the IOC was seen) to get the final score. Full audit trail, including the one sanity-gate failure this mapping hit and how it was fixed, is in docs/severity_derivation.md.
